What to add next

  1. Graph questions. Show a graph of f and pick f′ from four drawings. Covers A2.1, A2.2, B1.1, B1.4 and B1.5, the largest cluster of gaps, and tests reward it heavily.
  2. Systems of lines and planes. Two planes meeting in a line, three planes solved by elimination, and lines that meet, run parallel or are skew. Covers C3.1, C3.2, C4.4 and the rest of C4.7.
  3. Rational and radical derivatives. √x as x1/2, (x² + 1)(x − 1)−1, √(x² + 5). Completes A3.4, A3.5 and B1.3.
  4. 2D vectors with bearings. N 40° W, polar to Cartesian, resultant forces. Covers C1.2 and C1.3.
  5. Plane equations in every form, and ln x as the inverse of eˣ. Covers C4.5, C4.6 and A2.7.

Not fixable in a multiple-choice app: written solutions and communication, which Ontario marks as their own categories. Those need paper and a tutor.
