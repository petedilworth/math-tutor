Seven Threads to Calculus

Three curriculum documents, 187 separate expectations, and about seven ideas. Each one runs the whole length of school mathematics, changing its name at every stage. The documents are organized to be audited, so they hide the through-line. This is the through-line.

Select a thread to follow it. Two of them do not run the whole way, and those gaps are the most useful thing on the page.

Twelve questions that test understanding

Not procedures, reasons. The curriculum asks none of these directly. If you can answer them in language a ten-year-old follows, you understand this material better than most people who teach it.

Sources. The Grade 11 and 12 columns are transcribed from The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 11 and 12: Mathematics, 2007 (revised), and the expectation codes shown are that document's own.

What is not sourced. The Grade 3 to Grade 10 columns are my reading of where each idea first appears, not transcribed expectations. Grades 1 to 8 run on a 2020 document, Grade 9 on MTH1W from 2021, Grade 10 on a 2005 document. Treat those five columns as a sketch until they are checked against the real text.
